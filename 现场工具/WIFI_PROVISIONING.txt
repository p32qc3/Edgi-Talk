EDGI PC WiFi setup

1. Connect the board to the computer with the debugger USB cable.
2. Before first use, set OPENOCD_EXE and OPENOCD_SCRIPTS to your local
   Infineon OpenOCD executable and scripts directory.
3. Double-click wifi_provision.cmd.
4. Enter the WiFi name and password.
5. Wait for the success message.

The successful WiFi is saved on the board. Future power-ups reconnect
automatically. Run the tool again at any time to switch to another WiFi.
